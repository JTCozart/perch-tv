What it does

A security monitor, minus the monitor

Built for the remote and the couch, not squeezed down from a phone app.

Up to 16 cameras a page

The grid grows from one camera to four by four, and more cameras go on extra pages instead of shrinking past readable.

Wired RTSP, straight from the camera

Mains-powered Tapo cameras and other RTSP cameras play directly over your network. Tiles use each camera's light stream; full screen switches to full quality.

Room for battery and solar cameras

Tapo battery and solar cameras offer no RTSP, so Perch talks to them the way the Tapo app does at home, with your Tapo app password. Experimental. Any stream a bridge publishes (go2rtc, Home Assistant, an NVR) works too.

Reconnects by itself

When a camera drops off Wi-Fi or reboots, its tile says so and keeps trying every few seconds, so the wall heals without you.

Knows what your TV can handle

TV boxes can only decode so many videos at once. Perch asks yours, plays as many tiles live as it can, and pauses the rest until you select one.

Passwords stay on the TV

Camera passwords are encrypted with a key held in the TV's own secure storage. There is no account to sign up for and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Compatibility

Which cameras work

Perch plays standard RTSP and HLS streams. What decides it is whether your camera offers one.

CameraWorks with PerchHow
Wired or plug-in Tapo cameras (for example C100, C200, C210, C310, C320WS, C520WS) Yes, directly Turn on the camera account in the Tapo app, then add the camera's IP address in Perch. Dual-lens models show either lens.
Other RTSP cameras and NVRs Yes, directly Add the camera's RTSP address as a stream.
Tapo battery and solar cameras (C410, C420, C425, D230 and similar) Experimental These models offer no RTSP. Choose Tapo app password and Perch connects the way the Tapo app does on your home network. No sound yet, and a firmware update can stop it working. A bridge is the fallback.
Tapo D225, D235 and TD25 doorbells When hardwired TP-Link enables RTSP on these only when wired with the jumper cable and set to always on.

Model notes come from TP-Link's own RTSP and ONVIF FAQ and can change with firmware. Check your camera's support page if it is not listed. Perch is an independent app and is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with TP-Link. Tapo is a trademark of TP-Link.

Getting started

On the wall in about ten minutes

1

Install Perch

It is not in the Play Store, so you sideload it once with the free Downloader app or from your phone.

2

Create a camera account

In the Tapo app, give each camera a camera account. It is separate from your Tapo login and only works on your network.

3

Add your cameras

Enter each camera's name, IP address and camera account on the TV. They appear on the wall right away.

4

Sit back

Select any tile for full screen with sound. Left and right step through your cameras; Back returns to the wall.

Questions

Good to know

Does Perch record video?

No. Perch is a live viewer. Keep recording where it happens today: the camera's SD card, a hub or an NVR.

Can I watch my cameras away from home?

Perch talks to cameras on your home network. Away from home, connect the TV to your network over a VPN. Opening camera ports to the internet is not recommended.

Why are some tiles paused?

Every TV has a limit on how many videos it can decode at once. Perch plays as many as yours supports and pauses the rest. Select a paused tile to watch it full screen.

How does Perch play a solar camera with no RTSP?

Tapo cameras also speak TP-Link's own protocol on your home network, the one the Tapo app uses when you are at home. Perch signs in to it with your Tapo app password. TP-Link does not document or support it, so treat it as experimental: a firmware update can change it. A battery camera kept on a live wall wakes up and stays awake, so it uses far more battery than usual.

Does it work on Fire TV or an Android TV box?

Perch runs on Google TV and Android TV 6.0 or newer, which includes most Android TV boxes. Fire TV devices can sideload it the same way; the install guide covers it.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no ads, no account and no subscription.
